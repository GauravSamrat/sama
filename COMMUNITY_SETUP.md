# Community setup

Set entries in studio.js community or social. Supply a real HTTPS destination, label, platform, kind and scope, then set enabled to true. Use general for the Community page or a product ID for a product invitation. Use a verified studio address in studio.js email for public email contact. Missing or disabled channels display no join action.

Confirm the destination is accessible to the intended audience and add the community guidelines to the group. Users choose whether to join. Disable a channel if its invite expires or moderation is unavailable.
