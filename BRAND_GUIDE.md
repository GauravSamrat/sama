# SAMA brand guide

SAMA is the provisional studio name. The wordmark is uppercase with letterspacing and a companion mark formed by two intersecting orbits. The voice is composed and direct. Tell readers what they can do and what is available. Give limitations the same care as descriptions.

The homepage pairs an editorial serif with a system sans-serif. Hindi and Bengali use script-appropriate system fonts and looser headings. The studio palette uses forest green, pale paper and muted green-gray. Citta uses moss and warm paper, SAHAJA uses amber and terracotta, and Supreme Council uses slate blue. Light and dark tokens live in styles.css.

The mark also serves as the SVG favicon. The shared 1200 × 630 PNG and orbital hero are in assets/brand. If the provisional name changes, update studio.js and remake the share image to match. Domain and trademark availability are pending independent verification.
