# Abundance brand guide

Abundance uses the supplied interwoven infinity-loop artwork. The hero uses a web-sized crop of the full wordmark. Header and footer use its symbol crop. The favicon simplifies the weave to thicker strokes so the loop can be read at small sizes. The light backing preserves the artwork’s colors in either theme. The voice is composed and direct.

The homepage pairs an editorial serif with a system sans-serif. Hindi and Bengali use script-appropriate system fonts and looser headings. The studio palette uses forest green, pale paper and muted green-gray. Citta uses moss and warm paper, SAHAJA uses amber and terracotta, and Supreme Council uses slate blue. Light and dark tokens live in styles.css.

The 192 × 192 PNG doubles as the manifest icon and social profile asset. The shared 1200 × 630 PNG and the hero JPEG are in assets/brand. Domain and trademark availability are pending independent verification.
