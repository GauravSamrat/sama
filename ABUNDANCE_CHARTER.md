# Abundance charter

More possibilities. For everyone.

We begin by listening to people. We build thoughtfully and improve with feedback. Useful technology can give people time, understanding, independence and more ways to participate. Our immediate work is to make learning, planning and evaluating ideas easier, and to make the tools and guides available without an account.

Abundance is independent. Community participation is voluntary and replies depend on capacity. We welcome constructive criticism and make availability and limitations clear. Professional services can fund ongoing work through agreed scopes and quotes.
