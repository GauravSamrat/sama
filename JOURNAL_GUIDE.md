# Journal guide

The Journal is the site's articles and blog section. Edit locales/journal.js. Use a unique URL-safe id, titles, summaries, category and paragraph rows in English, Hindi and Bengali. Set related product IDs through products. Optional date and updated fields take ISO calendar dates. Reading time and links to related entries are generated.

The initial first-person drafts are for founder review. Editorial preview is enabled locally. Draft pages display a review label, use noindex and stay out of the sitemap. Mark approved entries reviewed and set editorialPreview to false before a public release. Leave dates empty until you know the actual publication dates.

If a translation is incomplete, the full article appears in English with a visible language notice. Review native-language wording before publishing. Future topics include accessible local-first software, lessons from beta testing and changes made through community feedback.
