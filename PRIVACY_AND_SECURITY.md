# Privacy and security

The site has no analytics, accounts or location requests. Browser storage remembers appearance and language. Problem draft text and consent choices can remain in tab session storage. Email is excluded from those drafts. Clear a draft on a shared device when you finish. Public issue trackers can be read by others.

Firebase serves static files and cannot receive a private form POST by itself. Delivery is disabled until an approved endpoint is configured. Keep its admin access private. Before launching collection, publish the actual provider, retention period, contact for deletion and access, and processing purpose. A proposed starting point is to delete closed submissions after 90 days unless continued retention is agreed and documented.

Keep credentials and personal submissions out of static assets, logs, public repositories and archives. Do not send private submissions to AI services without a separate disclosed and authorized workflow.
